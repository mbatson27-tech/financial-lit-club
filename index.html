Our Mission

The Financial & Economic Literacy Club is dedicated to giving every member, no matter their age or experience, a real understanding of how money works. We teach the skills that schools often leave out: budgeting, saving, investing, understanding credit, and making smart financial decisions. By learning from each other and passing that knowledge on to younger students, we aim to develop confident, responsible, and financially independent individuals who are prepared to build wealth, avoid costly mistakes, and make informed choices for the rest of their lives.
